Load rule

Pick a weight you could lift about 25 times, then stop at 15. Nothing through the trunk above roughly 40 kg. Progress by adding reps rather than weight — build 12 up to 20, then a small increment and back to 12.

Cool down — every session

Five minutes of easy walking or cycling, then two minutes on your feet before you sit down.

No sauna, steam room or hot tub for at least half an hour afterwards.
